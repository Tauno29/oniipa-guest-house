# Oipapa Guesthouse — Design Direction

## Three stylistic approaches

### Theme Name: Desert Editorial
Very quiet, tactile hospitality design with generous negative space, warm neutrals, and a magazine-like image rhythm. It should feel considered, local, and easy to trust.
**Probability:** 0.07

### Theme Name: Modern Homestead
A more practical, sunlit direction using structured cards, earthy colors, and friendly utility-led details. It would feel approachable and family-run without becoming rustic or overly literal.
**Probability:** 0.03

### Theme Name: Night Arrival
A darker, cinematic direction built around dusk photography, deep umber surfaces, and restrained brass accents. It would feel intimate and atmospheric, like a late check-in with the porch light on.
**Probability:** 0.09

## Selected approach: Desert Editorial

### Design Movement
Contemporary African editorial hospitality: a blend of quiet luxury, Swiss-inspired restraint, and tactile travel print layouts rooted in northern Namibian earth tones.

### Core Principles
1. **Space is hospitality.** Let the page breathe so the guesthouse feels calm before the visitor arrives.
2. **Local, not themed.** Use the real uploaded photography and grounded color references rather than decorative safari clichés.
3. **Editorial rhythm.** Alternate image-led moments with precise, practical booking information.
4. **Warm utility.** Every interaction should make finding a room, understanding the stay, or making contact feel simple.

### Color Philosophy
The palette is built from sun-washed plaster, dry grass, warm sand, and espresso shadow. Cream keeps the interface open and gentle; clay brown carries the brand's sense of place; dark espresso provides readable contrast and a feeling of permanence. The ownable accent is **Oipapa Clay (#B98568)**, used sparingly for calls to action and small moments of warmth.

### Layout Paradigm
A left-anchored editorial canvas with an asymmetric hero, offset image frames, and thin rules that feel like a printed travel journal. Sections should alternate between wide breathing room and compact information bands rather than defaulting to centered marketing blocks.

### Signature Elements
- Thin espresso rules and small terracotta square markers, echoing the uploaded flyers.
- Overlapping image frames with off-white borders, used as a visual cue for collected memories.
- Small uppercase labels with generous tracking, paired with high-contrast serif display headlines.

### Interaction Philosophy
Interactions are quiet and decisive: clear hover underlines, compact sliding cues, and prominent direct-contact actions. The page never shouts; it guides guests toward the next practical step.

### Animation
Use short, soft ease-out transitions under 300ms. Image frames lift by a few pixels on hover, links reveal a terracotta underline, and the hero content enters with a staggered opacity/translate reveal. Respect reduced motion by disabling non-essential transforms.

### Typography System
Use **Cormorant Garamond** for display headlines and room names, with **DM Sans** for body copy, labels, prices, and navigation. Display type should be large but not oversized; body copy should stay compact and highly legible. Uppercase labels use DM Sans at 0.12em tracking.

### Brand Essence
Oipapa Guesthouse is a clean, comfortable self-catering stay in Obuno for travelers who want a calm base with the practical warmth of a local home. Personality: **grounded, gracious, unhurried**.

### Brand Voice
Headlines sound observant and assured; CTAs sound direct and human; microcopy answers the guest's next question without filler.

Example lines:
- “A quiet place to land in northern Namibia.”
- “Call ahead, arrive easy.”

### Wordmark & Logo
Use the generated doorway-inspired geometric mark as the visual signature, paired with a typeset wordmark that gives “Oipapa” a refined editorial presence. Keep the mark bold and legible in small sizes; never reduce it to a generic circle or default icon.

### Signature Brand Color
**Oipapa Clay — #B98568.** A sun-warmed terracotta that feels specific to the property and the landscape around it.

## Style Decisions
- Use uploaded photos as the primary proof of place, not as decorative background filler.
- Keep all booking details visible and phone/email-first because the intended guest journey is direct and offline-friendly.
- Do not use fake reviews, ratings, or testimonials.

## Delivery note
The website is a frontend-only hospitality landing page based on the supplied guesthouse images and contact details; booking remains direct by phone or email.
